35th Charter Anniversary

Celebrating 35 years of purpose, leadership, and legacy

Every milestone tells a story—and ours is one of passion, resilience, and a shared commitment to making a difference.

For 35 years, the Rotaract Club of Rizal Technological University has been home to leaders who dared to serve, dream, and create meaningful change beyond themselves. Through every project, partnership, and person we’ve touched, our mission has remained constant: to uplift communities and inspire the next generation of changemakers.

35 years of Service Above Self, and steadfast in our commitment to creating lasting impact.

As we celebrate this remarkable journey, we honor the members, alumni, advisers, and partners whose dedication has shaped our legacy. While we take pride in how far we’ve come, we are even more excited for the future we will continue to build—together.

Here’s to 35 years of transforming compassion into action, challenges into opportunities, and vision into impact.

Happy 35th Charter Anniversary, Rotaract Club of Rizal Technological University!

Outgoing President

Thank you and congratulations, Good President Jeoffrey Castillejo!

As this Rotary year comes to an end, we would like to thank and congratulate our Good President Jeoffrey Castillejo for his unwavering dedication and for inspiring the club through service throughout his term.

Moreover, our Outgoing Club President wants to share a short message of gratitude and reflection:

As outgoing officers of the club, we may be stepping down from our positions, but we assure and remain dedicated to extending our guidance, support, and cheering you guys on in all your future endeavors. We wish you all the best!

— GP Jeoffrey Castillejo

Pistang Pambata 2026 · August 15, 2026

Pistang Masaya, para sa mga bata

A day filled with kulay, saya, at ngiti!

Last August 15, 2026, the Rotaract Club of RTU joined hands with the Rotaract Club of Inspiring Leaders (RAC ILAYA) to bring “Pistang Pambata 2026: Kulay. Saya. Ngiti.” to life.

The event was filled with interactive programs that engaged the children through games, storytelling, and creative workshops designed to spark imagination and learning. Alongside these were recreational activities such as group activities and Filipino traditional games that encouraged teamwork, movement, and activeness.

At the end of every game, the children received different gifts as a token of care and encouragement. Giving to children is more than an act of charity—it is an investment in their sense of worth, belonging, and hope.

These small gestures create lasting impact, reminding children that they are seen, valued, and loved.

Through this collaboration, the Rotaract Club of RTU and RAC ILAYA demonstrated how youth-led service can create joyful and impactful experiences that inspire the spirit of the community and help bring brighter futures for the next generation.

Cultural Exchange · July 3, 2026

Ni Hao! Apa khabar! Hello! Kumusta!

Three greetings. Three cultures. One unforgettable afternoon of learning, sharing, and friendship.

Last July 3, from 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM, the “Kuya Center” became a vibrant space where cultures came together through a meaningful “Cultural Exchange Program” — a joint initiative of the Rotary Club of Mandaluyong and the Rotary Club of Pudu, Malaysia.

The participants explored the richness of Chinese culture through a basic introduction to the Mandarin language, experienced basic martial arts, and enjoyed the colorful tradition and symbolism behind the Dragon Dance.

To complete the cultural exchange, we proudly showcased one of the Philippines’ most beloved traditional folk dances — the Tinikling — sharing with our guests the rhythm, resilience, and spirit of Filipino heritage.

Most importantly, this meaningful activity was held at the Kuya Center, a place that provides a safe and nurturing home for orphaned and underprivileged children. More than being a venue, the Kuya Center is a community that empowers young lives through love, care, education, and opportunities to grow. Seeing the children’s smiles and enthusiasm made the experience even more meaningful for everyone involved.

Because cultural exchange isn’t just about learning a new language or discovering new traditions — it’s about opening our hearts, building lasting friendships, and creating opportunities that inspire hope and understanding across cultures.

From “Ni Hao” to “Hello” to “Kumusta,” every greeting became a reminder that despite our different backgrounds, we all speak the universal language of kindness.
