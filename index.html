Where Potential Turns Into Purpose.

The Rotaract Club of Rizal Technological University (RAC-RTU) brings together students who aspire to grow as leaders, build meaningful connections, and make a difference beyond the classroom. Through service, fellowship, and shared experiences, members discover their strengths and turn their potential into purposeful action.

As part of the global Rotaract community, RAC-RTU connects young leaders to opportunities that extend beyond campus. Through collaborative initiatives and community-centered projects, the club upholds the principle of Service Above Self, fostering compassion, encouraging collective action, and creating meaningful change in the communities it serves.

Rotary International
Rotary International is a global humanitarian service organization dedicated to bringing people together to create positive and lasting change. Through its Rotaract program, Rotary extends its mission of service to young leaders, providing opportunities to develop leadership, build meaningful connections, and take part in community initiatives. Together, Rotary and Rotaract form a worldwide network united by the principle of Service Above Self.
Rotary Club of Mandaluyong
The Rotary Club of Mandaluyong is the sponsoring club of the Rotaract Club of Rizal Technological University. As part of the Rotary network, it provides guidance, support, and opportunities for collaboration, helping young Rotaractors strengthen their leadership, deepen their commitment to service, and contribute to meaningful community initiatives.
Rizal Technological University
Rizal Technological University serves as the home of the RAC-RTU, bringing together students from various colleges. As a university-based organization, RAC-RTU provides students with opportunities to develop their leadership, strengthen their sense of community, and extend their learning beyond the classroom through fellowship and meaningful service.

The Four-Way Test

Composed of four simple yet meaningful questions, it encourages us to practice honesty, fairness, goodwill, and service in our everyday decisions. For RAC-RTU, this is a guiding principle in every decision, every action, and every act of service.

Is it the TRUTH?

Honesty and integrity begin with choosing what is truthful in our words, decisions, and actions.

Is it FAIR to all concerned?

Fairness means respecting different perspectives, treating others with consideration, and making decisions without prejudice.

Will it build GOODWILL and BETTER FRIENDSHIPS?

Every interaction is an opportunity to strengthen trust, foster understanding, and build meaningful relationships.

Will it be BENEFICIAL to all concerned?

Meaningful service considers the well-being of everyone involved and seeks outcomes that create lasting value for the community.
