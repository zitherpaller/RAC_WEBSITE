Our Roots

Rooted in Service: Club History

Established on July 1, 1991, in the aftermath of the eruption of Mount Pinatubo, the Rotaract Club of Rizal Technological University (RAC-RTU) has grown into one of the longstanding clubs of Rotary International District 3800. For 35 years, the club has remained committed to developing young leaders, fostering meaningful connections, and creating opportunities to serve communities through compassion and collective action.

The club was pioneered by Dr. Norma Mendez, a former professor at Rizal Technological University who planted the seeds of service within the university. Her vision was carried forward by Niño Pacia Maliban, a dedicated Social Studies professor whose commitment helped nurture the club’s continuing journey. Under the sponsorship of the Rotary Club of Mandaluyong, RAC-RTU has strengthened its ties with its sponsoring club through collaborative initiatives, service projects, and activities grounded in the principle of Service Above Self.

In its earlier years, membership in RAC-RTU was primarily limited to students pursuing a Bachelor of Secondary Education major in Social Studies (BSEd Social Studies). Over time, the club expanded its reach and opened its doors to students from various colleges and academic programs across Rizal Technological University, both Mandaluyong and Pasig campus. This development allowed the club to embrace a more diverse community of aspiring leaders, bringing together individuals with different disciplines, perspectives, and experiences under one shared commitment to service.

Throughout its history, RAC-RTU has provided a platform for students to discover their potential as leaders and advocates for change. Through humanitarian initiatives, community engagement, and various service endeavors, the club has sought to address pressing concerns, inspire civic responsibility, and encourage its members to become active contributors to society. Beyond the projects it carries out, RAC-RTU cultivates an understanding that meaningful service begins with empathy, grows through collaboration, and creates lasting change through consistent action.

The club’s continuing commitment to service is also reflected in Lila’t Liwanag — “From the color we carry, to the light we leave behind.” Inspired by the color theme of the current Rotary Year of the club, this concept symbolizes the shared purpose of carrying forward the club’s legacy while creating a meaningful difference in the lives of others. It represents the belief that leadership goes beyond recognition and that service becomes truly meaningful when it brings hope, inspires others, and contributes to positive change.

Building upon 35 years of shared experiences, dedication, and collective service, RAC-RTU continues to honor the contributions of the generations that came before it while welcoming new leaders who will carry its mission forward. Guided by Service Above Self and inspired by the Rotary Year theme, Create Lasting Impact, the club remains committed to empowering young leaders, strengthening communities, and contributing to a better future for all.

As its mantra declares:“RAC-RTU is ready to strive to break a boundary, rebuild a relationship with other people with intimacy, and ignite the hope with consistency in creating and shaping the future of humanity through Quality and Pinoymenal Advocacy!”

Club Vision

The Rotaract Club of Rizal Technological University envisions to be a pro-active Rotaract club organization that molds and enhances students of Rizal Technological University to be a leader beyond positions and catalyst of change in the Glocal perspective in serving the school and the community.

Club Mission

In achieving and attaining the Vision, the Rotaract Club of Rizal Technological University is committed to fulfilling and imposing Rotary Vision, objectives, and principles in all aspects as exposing our heart to service. The Rotaract Club of Rizal Technological University is committed to creating and developing sustainable development projects and programs that will help and benefit the school and community.

Club Characteristic

  • Visionary
  • Glocal Leaders
  • Service Oriented
  • Inclusive
  • Versatile
  • Goal Digger

Club Goals

To create and encourage students to know and learn about Rotaract. To create a family whose hearts are eager to lead in school and extend help in a community.

Club Objectives

By abiding in the club’s Vision and Mission, Rotaract Club of Rizal Technological University will impose and passionately open a door of opportunities in creating impactful initiatives, will serve to change lives and will elevate leadership towards a better RTURactor in our school, community, district and country.

Our Impact

The four avenues of service guide everything we do.

Club Service

Running the club itself: officer roles, meetings, and the culture that keeps members coming back.

Community Service

Local outreach in Mandaluyong, from clean-up drives to donation and tutoring programs.

International Service

Joint projects and friendships with Rotaract clubs abroad.

Professional Development

Workshops, mentorship, and networking that sharpen skills you’ll carry beyond graduation.
